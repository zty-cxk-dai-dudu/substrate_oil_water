# Data availability

Processed simulation data, representative atomic structures and molecular dynamics trajectories, and trained potential-energy models are available at https://github.com/zty-cxk-dai-dudu/substrate_oil_water (version 0.6.1). Numerical source data are organised by figure in the `data` directory.

# Code availability

The scripts used for molecular dynamics simulations and computational analyses are available at https://github.com/zty-cxk-dai-dudu/substrate_oil_water.
